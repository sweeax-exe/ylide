# Code of conduct

Ylide's community lives on GitHub and on the
[Ylide Discord](https://discord.gg/GRzTvRGHpr). We want both to be places where
anyone can ask, report and contribute without being put down, whatever their
experience, background or language.

We follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

* Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and people writing in a
  language that is not their first.
* Criticise code and ideas, not people. Accept that a maintainer may decide
  differently than you would.
* No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised content, doxxing or
  threats, in public or in private messages.
* Do not use Ylide's spaces to promote unrelated products or to share malware,
  pirated content or other people's private data.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, open a private ticket on the Discord (channel
`#tickets`, "Something else"). Only you and the team can see it. You can also
report a single comment or post with the platform's own report button.

The maintainers handle every report confidentially. Depending on what
happened, they may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban
someone from the GitHub repository and the Discord. Decisions follow the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.

This code applies in all of Ylide's spaces and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.
